The urinalysis test strip for Patient 2 demonstrates pronounced pathological abnormalities consistent with acute urinary tract inflammation. The upper parameters show negative readings for glucose (light cyan), bilirubin (light tan), and ketones (pale buff), alongside a yellow-green specific gravity pad reflecting normal urine concentration. In contrast, the blood parameter pad turns a dark navy blue, indicating strong positivity for hematuria (+++). The pH pad registers an orange-yellow color corresponding to acidic urine around pH 5.0 to 5.5, while protein remains at a negative to trace light yellow-green and urobilinogen displays a normal light peach hue. Although the nitrite test pad remains off-white (negative), the bottom leukocyte esterase pad displays a intense deep purple coloration, confirming marked leukocyturia/pyuria (+++). This clinical combination of pronounced hematuria and pyuria in acidic urine strongly points toward a lower urinary tract infection (UTI), severe cystitis, or urolithiasis with associated mucosal tissue irritation.
