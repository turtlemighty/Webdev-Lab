Objectives

The primary objective of this automated analyzer is to deliver rapid, highly reliable biological evaluations without human color-interpretation bias. By converting subtle chemical shifts into precise digital metrics, the system minimizes clinical error margins to less than 0.5% across standardized urinalysis test panels.

Working Principle

The system operates using multi-wavelength optical reflectometry. Controlled LED light source reflects off reagent test pads after a specific immersion period. Integrated photo-sensors quantify light absorption shifts corresponding to target concentrations—such as glucose at 100 mg/dL or critical pH variations between 5.0 and 8.5.

Benefits

Clinicians benefit from instant data transmission directly into the patient's EHR, eliminating manual entry delays. Furthermore, automated tracking flags critical indicators like early-stage UTI markers or micro-hemorrhages instantaneously, accelerating critical point-of-care decisions when time-to-treatment is vital.

Clinical Endorsement

The instant EHR integration ensures zero transcription errors, allowing our medical team to focus immediately on target treatments." — Dr. Aris Thorne, Chief of Clinical Diagnostics
